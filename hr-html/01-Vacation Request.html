    Request's terms

    1. It must be after the lapse of 3 months from the date of employment (after the end of the first trial period)

    2. The employee deserves a regular annual leave of 21 days per year (the employee's share of vacation per month is 1.75 days). The leave is calculated in proportion to the period spent at work, and the employee does not take leave except after submitting an application to the administration and approval by them.

    3. The employee has the right to full vacation leave on holidays and occasions according to the following:

    - Four days on the occasion of Eid Al-Fitr, starting from the day following the 29th day of the blessed month of Ramadan, according to      Umm al-Qura calendar.

    - Four days on the occasion of Eid Al-Adha, starting from the day of standing in Arafat.

    - One day on the occasion of the Kingdom's national day.

    If the days of these vacations overlap with the weekly rest, the employee shall be compensated for by the equivalent before or after the days of these holidays.

    The worker is entitled to take full pay leave in the following cases:

    1- 5 days upon marriage.

    2- 3 days in the event of the birth of a newborn

    3- 5 days in the event of the death of the employee’s wife, one of his assets, or one of his branches.

    4- 4 months and 10 days in the event of the death of the husband of the Muslim employee, and she has the right to extend her without pay if she is pregnant until she gives birth to her pregnancy, and she is not allowed to take advantage of the rest of the waiting leave granted to her after giving birth to this pregnancy.

    5- 15 days in the event of the death of the husband of the non-Muslim employee, and the office has the right to request documents supporting the cases referred to

    But if the days of one of the two holiday days overlap with the national day vacation, the employee will not be compensated for his dementia.

    The employee - whose illness is proven with a medical certificate issued by an accredited medical center - is entitled to sick leave during one year, which begins from the date of the first sick leave, whether these procedures are related or intermittent, as follows:

    1- 30 days for the first full payment salary .

    2- 60 days following, by three quarters of the salary .

    3- 30 days after that, without payment of salary .

    The employee has the right to communicate his annual leave with sickness.
